Meet Your Clinician

Sarah Pickett, MS, CCC-SLP

Speech-Language Pathologist & Vocologist

Sarah is a speech-language pathologist and vocologist specializing in voice and upper airway disorders. She spent the past five years practicing in a multidisciplinary voice clinic alongside a fellowship-trained laryngologist, providing comprehensive care for individuals with complex voice conditions.

Recently, she returned home to Madison to begin her own private practice. With a background in musical theatre, Sarah brings both clinical expertise and artistic insight to her work and enjoys partnering with individuals across the lifespan to strengthen, restore, and optimize their voices.
