Meet Your Clinician

Sarah Pickett, MS, CCC-SLP

Voice-Specialized Speech-Language Pathologist

Sarah is a speech-language pathologist specializing in voice and upper airway disorders. She spent the past five years practicing in a multidisciplinary voice clinic alongside a fellowship-trained laryngologist, providing comprehensive care for individuals with complex voice conditions.

Recently, she returned home to Madison to begin her own private practice. With a background in musical theatre, Sarah brings both clinical expertise and artistic insight to her work and enjoys partnering with individuals across the lifespan to strengthen, restore, and optimize their voices.

Insurance & reimbursement information

Sarah Pickett Voice and Speech Services, LLC operates as a private pay practice. This model allows care to be tailored to your individual clinical needs.
